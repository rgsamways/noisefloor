## Technical

A truck roll is a physical, in-person visit to a customer's site (or a tower) by a technician, as opposed to remote troubleshooting. Because a truck roll has a real cost in technician time and travel, T1 support's job is to gather enough remote evidence — signal history, chain balance, cable test results, service-layer readings — to make the truck roll as targeted and productive as possible: arriving with a strong working theory of the fault (a specific cable segment, a specific piece of hardware) rather than starting the diagnosis from zero on site.

## Layman

This is when someone actually has to drive out and physically look at or touch the equipment, instead of fixing things remotely. It's the most expensive and slowest option, so a good remote diagnosis beforehand means the technician who drives out already has a good idea of what they're going to find and fix.
