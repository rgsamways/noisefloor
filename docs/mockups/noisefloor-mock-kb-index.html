Knowledge base

Definitions for every reading on the console. Claude-drafted, reviewed for accuracy — not vendor-specific, not tied to any case.

Radio link
Signal strengthdBm

How strong the received radio signal is at the antenna — more negative is weaker.

 Noise floordBm

The background RF noise a radio has to hear over. Rises with interference, not distance.

 SNRdB

Signal minus noise floor — the real measure of link health, not signal strength alone.

 Link quality%

A vendor-specific composite score (CCQ, link efficiency) — treat as approximate, not absolute.

 Modulation / MCSindex

How much data is packed into each symbol. Higher needs a cleaner link to hold.

 Chain imbalancedB

The gap between a radio's antenna chains. A bad cable or wet connector shows up here first.
Throughput & capacity
Airtime%

The field that explains capacity. Great signal plus saturated airtime is still slow.

 Channel utilization%

How busy the whole spectrum is, not just your link's share of it.

 Client countn

Sector capacity is shared — more clients means less airtime for everyone on it.
Service layer
DHCP lease—

A bad reading isn't always the radio. A dead or wrong lease looks like a link problem.

Where address translation happens, and why it matters for a customer's own troubleshooting.
